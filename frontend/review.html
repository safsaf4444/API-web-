How to read this forest plot:
Each dot represents one study. The horizontal whiskers show the 95% confidence interval (CI) — the range of plausible true effects. A narrow CI means a large, precise study; a wide CI means a small or uncertain one.

The diamond at the bottom is the pooled summary estimate across all studies. If it does not cross the vertical zero line, the treatment effect is statistically significant.

Dot size scales with sample size — larger dots carry more weight in the pooled estimate.

Fixed effects assumes all studies estimate the same underlying effect. Random effects allows for between-study heterogeneity and is more conservative when studies differ in design or population.
