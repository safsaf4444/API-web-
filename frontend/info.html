About Seren
A unified medical evidence platform for searching, synthesizing, and appraising research. Built for speed, transparency, and evidence-based practice.
Platform Access
Guest Mode
Search across Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, and Preprints instantly. Full-text Open Access loading and DOI resolution available without an account.
Registered Users
A free account unlocks the full research workflow:
• Save papers to your personal library & organise with folders
• AI summarisation, Q&A, and cross-paper synthesis
• Clinical appraisal, bias scoring, and trust metrics
• Notebook, community forum, and workspace tools
• BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) for premium AI models
Search
Multi-Source Search
Search All Sources mode fans out across six free academic APIs simultaneously — Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, and Preprints. Results are deduplicated by DOI, PMID, and normalised title, then cursor-paginated so you can load hundreds of thousands of hits progressively. The banner shows how many results have been loaded versus the total available across all sources.
Smart Filters
Filter by study type (auto-detected from title and abstract), year range, open access status, and language. Filters apply instantly to the current result set without re-fetching.
AI & Synthesis
Paper AI Panel
Open any paper and use the AI tab to summarise, ask questions, extract PICO, or request a clinical appraisal. Streaming responses via Server-Sent Events. AI explains figures, tables, and statistical outputs.
Cross-Paper Synthesis
Select papers from your library and run seven synthesis modes in one workspace:
• Narrative — unified prose summary across all selected papers
• Thematic — recurring themes and patterns identified
• Confounds — potential confounding variables flagged
• Outliers — results that diverge from consensus highlighted
• Sensitivity — how robust conclusions are to individual studies
• Sufficiency — whether the evidence base is adequate
• Research Questions — gaps the evidence does not yet answer
Evidence Drift
Tracks how findings and consensus have shifted across publication years for a topic, highlighting inflection points and reversals.
Trust & Validity
7-Pillar Trust System
Every AI synthesis output is scored across seven validity dimensions: Population generalisability, Intervention fidelity, Comparator appropriateness, Outcome relevance, Time horizon, Setting transferability, and Study Design strength. Scores combine into a single trust rating displayed alongside each synthesis.
Retraction Check
Papers are checked against the Europe PMC retraction index. A warning badge is shown if a paper has been retracted or has an expression of concern.
Analytics & Visualisation
Library Analytics
Study type breakdown, publication timeline, citation impact overview, bias heatmap across your saved papers, and a forest plot for quantitative comparisons.
Citation Network
Interactive graph showing citation relationships between papers in your library and their key references.
Workspace Tools
Statistical Calculator
Bedside toolkit for interpreting statistics in papers — runs entirely in your browser, nothing sent to a server:
• NNT — Number Needed to Treat from event rates
• Odds Ratio — 2×2 table input with 95% CI
• Cohen's d — standardised effect size with small/medium/large interpretation
Find it in the Tools page.
PDF Extraction & Figure Explainer
Upload a PDF to extract full text, figures metadata, and references. Upload a figure screenshot and the AI explains its clinical and statistical context.
Embedded Spreadsheet
A full FortuneSheet editor for manual data curation. Extracted PICO data can be imported directly. Auto-saves to your account.
AI Infrastructure
Included Free Tier
Gemini 1.5 Flash is the default — no API key required. If quota is reached, the system automatically falls back to Groq (Llama 3.3 70B). Both are free with no setup.
BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)
Set your own key on the AI settings page to use premium models. Auto-detected from key prefix:

• sk-ant- → Anthropic (Claude Haiku / Sonnet)
• AIza → Google (Gemini Pro)
• gsk_ → Groq (Llama 3.3)
• anything else → OpenAI (GPT-4o)

Keys are encrypted at rest. The frontend never stores your key in localStorage.
Ollama (Local)
Route AI to a local Ollama instance at localhost:11434. No data leaves your device.
Key Management
Check AI Key Status
Verify if your custom API key is linked to your account or clear it to return to the free tier.
